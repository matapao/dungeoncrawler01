# Phase 1 playcheck — small pieces and reference room

The one-player Alpha keeps the TD01 walking, orbit, zoom, directional animation and cosmetic hop.
From the hub, approach Friends and press F. Explore the 10 × 10 m reference room, then approach
the green marker by its return doorway and press F to return to the hub.

Check these before approving Phase 2:

- Do the native floor, wall, doorway, pillar and chest proportions feel useful around the hero?
- Can you walk through the open sample doorway and around the solid samples comfortably?
- Does the warm key, pale fill and violet backdrop feel close to the KayKit reference?
- Does Inspect room show the pieces clearly? Follow hero or move to resume normal following.
- Do repeated hub trips, mouse controls and the room cutaways feel predictable?

All specimens are inert. This phase adds no loot, combat, classes, multiplayer, generation, stairs
or vertical traversal. The lighting is a visual approximation, not recovered source-rig values.

Measured geometry, native pivots, licensing and blocker dimensions are recorded separately in
`PHASE1_ASSET_MEASUREMENTS.md`. Tests cover bounds, circular hero clearance, specimen collision,
the open passage, repeat travel and the existing TD01 controls. Browser screenshots are generated
in ignored `test-output/`. Verification and public release results are recorded in the handoff.

Pause here for the player's feedback. Phase 2 will combine the approved pieces into larger rooms,
corridors and intersections; Phase 3 assembly and multilevel work remain unimplemented.
