# Phase 3 playcheck — generated dungeon, two floors and cameras

The default **Two-floor expedition** is a small seeded dungeon assembled from the approved
KayKit rooms, corridors and intersections. Choose a seed in the hub, approach **Friends** and
press **F**. The seed and current area remain visible while exploring.

1. Keep seed **17031** for the first trip. Walk through the starting chamber and corridor,
   explore the intersection's branch, then find the stairs ahead.
2. Walk up the actual treads to **Floor 2** and continue to the marked upper lookout.
   Turn around and descend to Floor 1; there is no stair interaction button or teleport.
3. Use the **Camera** menu to compare **WoW follow**, **Diablo**, **Zelda** and **Top-down**.
   Each is playable with the same movement controls and hero position.
4. Select **Side slice** to inspect both labelled floors and their connecting stairs.
   Walking pauses in this view. Select a play camera to continue at the same position.
5. Return to the starting chamber's green marker and press **F** to reach the original hub.
6. Try **Next seed** or enter another number from 0 to 4294967295. Re-entering the same seed
   repeats its layout. Layout and seed changes are available in the hub.
7. Compare the retained **Reference pieces**, **Crossroads walk** and **Hall & corners** layouts
   if you want to check the lighting and walking against earlier phases.

WASD moves relative to the camera; Q/E or left/right arrows turn. Up/down arrows move forward/back.
Drag either mouse button to orbit in follow view; both buttons move forward. Wheel zooms in play
views and Space retains the cosmetic hop. Fixed camera presets keep their own elevation.

Check stair comfort in both directions, walls and pillars around the branch, visibility on each
floor, and camera switching without a jump in position. The return marker must work downstairs
only. Side inspection must resume without moving the hero. Held keys must clear during travel.

The neutral theme and approved lighting are retained. The generator is deliberately small and
bounded, with 64 recipe combinations and 32 footprints. There is one local player, no saved run,
combat, loot, keys, timer, class selection or party difficulty yet. The upper lookout gives no reward.

Pause here for the player's feedback before the next building phase.
